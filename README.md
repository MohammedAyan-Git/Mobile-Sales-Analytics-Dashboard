# Mobile-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard
A dynamic, interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze mobile phone sales performance across brands, models, cities, payment methods, customer ratings, months, and days. The dashboard transforms 3,835 mobile sales transactions into actionable business insights through dynamic KPIs and interactive visualizations.
---
## Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard
A dynamic, interactive data visualization tool built to explore mobile phone sales performance across India — focusing on sales trends, customer behavior, payment preferences, product performance, and geographic distribution.
---

## Purpose

The Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to help users explore and analyze mobile sales data across multiple Indian cities, brands, and models. The dashboard focuses on highlighting key sales metrics, customer satisfaction patterns, payment method trends, and seasonal demand fluctuations. This tool is intended for use by sales managers, retail analysts, business strategists, and data-driven decision-makers who seek to understand trends and performance drivers in the mobile retail market.

---

## Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- **Power BI Desktop** – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships established among tables (sales, customers, products, cities) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.
- **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews.

## Dataset used

- <a href="https://github.com/MohammedAyan-Git/Mobile-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard/blob/main/Mobile%20Sales%20Data.xlsx">Mobile Sales Data</a>

Data includes mobile phone sales transactions across major Indian cities, capturing details on:
- **Sales & Revenue:** Total sales amount, quantity sold, average price per unit
- **Product Info:** Brand, mobile model, and specifications
- **Customer Data:** Ratings, purchase dates, and city-level geography
- **Transaction Details:** Payment method (UPI, Debit Card, Cash, Credit Card), day of purchase, and monthly trends

## ✨ Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

The mobile retail industry in India is highly competitive, with numerous brands, models, and regional variations in consumer behavior. Sales teams, store managers, and analysts often lack a unified view to quickly answer critical questions such as:

- Which mobile models are driving the most revenue?
- How do sales fluctuate across months and days of the week?
- Which cities are the top-performing markets?
- What are customers saying — and how satisfied are they?
- Which payment methods are most preferred by consumers?

Raw transaction data alone makes it difficult to derive actionable insights efficiently.

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive visual tool that:

- Enables users to explore mobile sales performance across India.
- Supports decisions such as inventory planning, regional marketing, pricing strategy, and customer experience improvement.
- Uncovers trends in product popularity, seasonal demand, payment preferences, and customer satisfaction by region and time.

### • Business Impact & Insights

| Insight Area | Application |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Inventory Planning** | Stock more units of top-selling models (iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, Galaxy Note 20) ahead of peak months (March, July). |
| **Regional Marketing** | Focus promotional campaigns on high-performing cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore) while identifying growth opportunities in underperforming regions. |
| **Pricing Strategy** | Use average price data (40K) to benchmark and optimize pricing tiers across models and regions. |
| **Customer Experience** | Leverage high customer ratings (311 five-star reviews) to build trust and identify areas for improvement from lower ratings. |
| **Payment Optimization** | Ensure seamless UPI and digital payment infrastructure, while maintaining support for cash and card transactions. |
| **Staffing & Operations** | Align staffing and stock replenishment with peak sales days (Saturday, Monday) to maximize revenue capture. |
